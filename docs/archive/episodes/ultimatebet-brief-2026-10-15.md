## B. Story, evidence, and attribution rules

### Core verified findings

1. Summer 2007: David Paredes reported losses of about $70,000 to `NioNio`; shared hand histories revealed an unusually profitable run, including about $300,000 in one stretch. **Source:** Washington Post investigation, 2008-11-30.
2. AbsolutePoker's *separate* scandal in 2007 featured `POTRIPPER`; an accidentally supplied spreadsheet exposed other players' hidden-card information. It proved useful context for how poker communities investigated suspicious play. **Sources:** Washington Post, contemporary Casino City reporting, 2007–2008.
3. On 2008-01-12, UltimateBet was alerted to complaints about `NioNio`. The site's March 2008 statement acknowledged suspicious results; in May it acknowledged unfair play and said unauthorized code exposed other players' hole cards. **Sources:** UltimateBet statement reposted on Two Plus Two; PokerNews 2022 historical review.
4. According to the Kahnawake Gaming Commission's September 2009 decision, cheating spanned **June 2003–December 2007** and used **23 accounts / 117 usernames**. The commission concluded Russ Hamilton was *primarily responsible for and benefited from* the cheating. **Sources:** contemporaneous quotations from the regulator in CardPlayer, CasinoCity, PokerNewsDaily.
5. UltimateBet owner Tokwiro reimbursed **more than US$22 million** to affected players and paid **US$1.5 million in regulatory fines**, plus investigation costs. These values are **different categories**, not money pocketed by Russ Hamilton. **Sources:** Kahnawake-local reporting and contemporary analysis of final decision.
6. The regulator reported information concerning **31 associated individuals** to law enforcement. That does **not** establish 31 convictions or even identical roles in the cheating. **Source:** CardPlayer/KGC quotations.
7. In 2013, recordings from a meeting held in 2008 surfaced. Reporting on the recording describes Hamilton acknowledging taking money and a discussion of how refunds would be handled. **Source:** PokerNews, Pokerfuse (2013).
8. UltimateBet/AbsolutePoker also faced wider operational consequences following **April 15, 2011**, the US online-poker enforcement action. These later frozen account funds and reimbursement processes are a separate story and **must not be added to the $22M cheating refunds**. **Source:** PokerNews 2016 and further reporting.

### What the script deliberately avoids

- No invented exact hand, exact dialogue, gambling results, IP address, coded screen, courtroom verdict or FBI arrest in this case.
- Do not say *all* 117 usernames were used by 117 people, or that the platform's full code-level exploit was independently inspected by us.
- Do not imply Hamilton was criminally convicted in this matter. His lawyer publicly denied the allegations in 2008; the regulatory findings and later audio must be attributed distinctly.
- Avoid borrowing competitors' prose, scripts, thumbnails or footage. Public historical **facts and stories** can be researched and independently told.
- If using a generic Pexels computer screen as illustration, do not portray it as an actual 2008 screenshot from UltimateBet. Keep fabricated interface as clearly styled illustration.
